# Welcome

This site explores Hopfield Networks - a fascinating intersection of neuroscience, physics, and machine learning.

## About

This notebook demonstrates experiments with Hopfield Networks, named after the American computer scientist [John Hopfield](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/John_Hopfield) who won the 2024 physics Nobel Prize for his research on the subject.
